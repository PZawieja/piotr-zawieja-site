How I think about the work

Start with a clear definition. Then make it useful.

Good analytics starts before the dashboard. It starts with shared definitions, well-modelled data, checks that catch meaningful failures, and a clear path from a question to a trusted answer.

That foundation is also what makes AI useful in analytics. I’m especially interested in agents that work from governed semantic layers, carry the right context, ask when a question is ambiguous, and can be evaluated against known expectations.

My writing and open-source projects explore these ideas with synthetic examples and reusable engineering patterns.
